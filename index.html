How it works

  1. Pick course, regulation and (for B.Tech) branch.
  2. Open a semester and enter subject names, credits and grades.
  3. SGPA = Σ(credits × grade point) ÷ Σ credits.
  4. Save the semester. CGPA = total grade points ÷ total credits of the included saved semesters.
  5. Percentage is shown only when you provide a conversion factor from your official rules.

About

AKTUCalc helps students estimate SGPA, CGPA and percentage. SGPA is the credit-weighted grade average of one semester; CGPA is the cumulative equivalent. Grade points and conversion factors live in a central configuration and are labelled unverified until checked against official AKTU documents. Results are estimates, not official. This site is not affiliated with AKTU. Report errors by opening an issue in the project repository.
